# Question

How can PromiseGrid teach a concrete v0 profile in which heterogeneous embodiments remain one logical app, publish honest partial-conformance claims, preserve signing-key continuity across browser and helper hosts, and publish durable audit milestones for off-grid live activity without pretending the live channel is already a PromiseGrid binding?

Open decision points:

- What minimum fields in the App Manifest and Embodiment Claim are required for third-party interop review?
- When browser and helper cannot co-sign an Identity Continuity Receipt, what witness or break-witness is sufficient?
- Should the Savepoint Audit Envelope normalize one cited-object shape, or allow snapshot, save-blob, receipt, and break-witness variants under one envelope schema?
- What minimum guide-safe wording should state that a hash is an address to content identity, not by itself a retention, authorization, or availability promise?
